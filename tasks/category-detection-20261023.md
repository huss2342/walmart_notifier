# Category detection improvements — October 23, 2026

The owner requested better category detection, particularly clothing in Other or
Pets, with matching Discord support for any added category. This change uses the
existing eleven categories and channels; no new category is necessary.

## Findings and implementation

- The classifier lacked common clothing nouns: sweatshirt, sweatpants, romper,
  jumpsuit, bodysuit, vest, cardigan, hats, gloves, sunglasses and others.
- The phrase `pet carrier` incorrectly described a women's sweatshirt with a
  `pet carrier pocket`. That occurrence is now excluded from pet evidence while
  a real carrier elsewhere in the title still matches.
- Explicit pet garments and recovery suits go to Pets. Human animal graphics,
  adult-human labels and dog/cat mom/dad clothing remain Clothing; a stated pet
  recipient can still override references to a human owner. Printed pet garments
  remain Pets.
- More specific product-purpose phrases protect animal-themed decorations,
  storage racks, cosmetic/work/cleaning gloves, bottle boots, plumbing tees,
  car visor organizers and similar collisions. Actual unknowns remain Other;
  unrelated sweep queries still cannot invent evidence.
- Observed fuel-filter and console-cover misses are now Tools/Auto; selected
  obvious kitchen, home and electronics phrases also have coverage.

Changes are limited to `discord_bridge/classification.py`, its new regression
file, and documentation. Existing collector projection, queue behavior and
channel IDs are reused.
