Commercial feasibility needs resolution

Walmart’s current terms restrict commercial use and automated collection. Discord also restricts monetized offerings centered on third-party promotion. Obtain a permitted source and confirm the offering’s eligibility before public sales. These are unresolved dependencies, not approval findings.

Evidence, not a demand forecast

Adjacent services anchor the price test.

No directly comparable paid Walmart reviewer alert service with independently verified demand was established in the targeted search. Amazon Vine tools are substitutes for attention and workflow, not the same inventory or audience.

Ultraviner

Confirmed offer: alerts, rules and Discord integrations. Indexed official pricing lists Basic $4.99, Turbo $9.99 and Ultimate $14.99 monthly.

Buyer appeal: a broader toolset at an accessible monthly price. This puts pressure on a $20 alert-only proposition.

Where this project can separate: Walmart-specific discovery, portable links, relevance and clear feed health—once proved.

The live page loads some pricing dynamically. Reconfirm checkout. Published prices do not establish subscriber counts or willingness to pay for Walmart. Official product/pricing

SmartViner and free alternatives

Confirmed seller positioning: SmartViner advertises a free beta with filtering and alert features. Manual portal checking and existing community sharing remain alternatives.

Buyer appeal: little or no subscription cost. The paid feed must save effort and reduce irrelevant notifications.

Do not claim: superiority, guaranteed availability or proven paid demand. Vendor claims are not an independent performance comparison.

What to say, clarify and prove

Say: “Choose the reviewer items you care about and open them in your own Walmart account.”

Clarify: whether the buyer is already invited, which categories matter, how they currently find items, and how often delayed items remain useful.

Prove: link behavior, classification accuracy, useful alerts, delivery uptime, actual payment and renewal.

Avoid: guaranteed claims, admission to Walmart’s program, exclusive stock, guaranteed savings, or reducing competition outside this server.

A small pilot answers the missing questions

  1. Recruit 10–20 existing invited reviewers after source/eligibility checks.
  2. Run a 14-day private pilot with cancelable paid beta plans.
  3. Collect voluntary unavailable/claimed/not-eligible feedback and search time saved.
  4. Look for ten actual paying users and six second-month renewals before expanding. These are proposed gates, not industry benchmarks.

One durable pipeline

Browser observation→Normalize & tag→SQLite transaction→Destination jobs→Discord worker

Acknowledge durable intake, then send independently by destination. Retries, rate limits and five-minute delays must survive restarts. Telegram failure must not stop discovery before paid launch.

Do not queue the old history as new alerts. Live availability and listing publication times are not known. Verification pauses remain manual.

Links and categories need evidence

The supplied sampling link has contextual parameters and no obvious account token. Preserve an allowlisted review URL alongside the regular product URL, and test it with a second invited account.

Classify each item using department evidence, title rules and overrides. A search query is a hint: “wood” is not a product department.

The browser connector could not access the user’s Chrome session. Cross-account and mobile behavior remain untested. Share neither the owner’s session nor claims allowance.

Keep launch scope small

  1. Resolve source permission and test links.
  2. Build one private durable Discord alert.
  3. Add item tagging, value routing and visible feed health.
  4. Prove permissions, delays, stale handling and restarts.
  5. Connect eligible billing; run the capped paid pilot.
  6. Add opt-in personal DMs if demand warrants them.

Native server billing is a candidate if the creator and offering qualify. Paid app features can have separate Premium Apps obligations. External checkout is not automatic policy clearance.

Source posture

Local evidence: current extension and Python source, active saved filters, 58,108 snapshot item rows plus 12,291 appended item rows; marker events excluded. Counts reconcile to 70,399 distinct IDs with no malformed log lines or overlap. Recent Docker logs establish successful value alerts, connection failures and verification pauses.

Limitations: account-specific, retained observation history; 29,133 explicit variants; 540 zeros. Titles can be truncated. No full URL, image, department, last-seen or stock history is retained in this ledger. Seven-day and 14-day slices end at the latest record, not at report generation.

Not verified: permission for commercial reuse, Discord acceptance of the offering, current checkout totals, customer payment behavior, cross-account claim eligibility, complete catalog coverage or live stock.

CRM, internal messaging and warehouse integrations were not relevant. No customer payment data or user Walmart credentials were collected.
