Privacy

Updated November 12, 2026

This page describes the public Reviewer Alerts website. Reviewer Alerts is an independent service for existing Walmart Recognized Reviewer participants.

This website

This static website has no account signup form, advertising pixel, analytics script, or tracking cookies added by Reviewer Alerts. We do not ask for your Walmart login, payment card, or other personal information here. Our hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and technical logs, to deliver and protect the website.

Discord links

All Join buttons use the same permanent Discord invitation. Older ad links may include campaign labels; this website ignores them and does not identify individual visitors, store those labels in cookies, or send them to an advertising pixel.

Discord and Whop

Choosing a Discord link takes you to Discord. Membership payments, if you choose Pro, are handled through Whop. Those services apply their own privacy policies and may collect account or payment information. Reviewer Alerts does not collect payment-card details on this website.

Server membership

Discord membership features use your Discord identity and membership status to provide access. Personal item requests require the request information you choose to submit so matching alerts can be delivered. Please do not share Walmart credentials or other sensitive information in the server.

Questions or requests

Contact the creator through the Reviewer Alerts Discord server or the creator’s Reddit profile about the service or your information.
