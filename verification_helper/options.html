Reviewer Verification Assistant

Automatically attempt supported Press & Hold checks on your signed-in Walmart reviewer pages.

Experimental: live reliability is not established. Readable completion feedback releases the button early; otherwise the assistant releases at 15 seconds. It waits for a usable catalogue before reporting recovery. A failed or uncertain attempt keeps monitoring paused.

This companion has Chrome debugger access, which is a broad permission. Its code restricts attachment and input to Walmart reviewer and blocked pages. It does not move your desktop mouse. Chrome may display a debugging banner; another debugger can prevent an attempt.

One attempt per observed episode, up to three per hour, at least one minute apart. Attempts are remembered across worker restarts. Disabling stops an active hold at its next check. No cookies, credentials, page text or screenshots are saved or sent to a service.
